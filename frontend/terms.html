1. The Protocol

Zorrito ("the Protocol") is a no-loss savings game deployed on the Celo blockchain. Users deposit USDT, which is supplied to Aave V3 on Celo to generate yield. That yield accumulates in a prize pool distributed to one randomly selected participant each year. Deposited principal is never put at risk by the Protocol's design — users can withdraw their deposit at any time.

The Protocol operates through immutable smart contracts. There is no admin key, no owner, and no party capable of pausing, upgrading, or draining the contracts. Interaction with the Protocol is direct and permissionless.
