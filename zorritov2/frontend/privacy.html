Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Zorrito is built on public blockchain infrastructure. By design, we collect as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what data we do and don't collect, and how it is used.

1. What We Don't Collect

Zorrito does not collect, store, or process:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any identity document
  • Passwords — wallet authentication is handled entirely by your wallet provider
  • Payment card or bank account information
  • Your IP address (beyond what is inherent in standard web requests to our CDN)
  • Location data beyond what your browser exposes by default

2. What We Collect

Data Purpose Stored by
Wallet address (public) Display your fox, streak, deposit, and leaderboard position Celo blockchain (public)
On-chain transaction data Deposits, feeds, withdrawals — permanent public record Celo blockchain (public)
Anonymous usage events Product analytics: page views, wallet connects, deposits, feeds PostHog (EU servers)
Language preference Remember your selected language (EN/ES/PT) Your browser's localStorage

3. Blockchain Data Is Public

All transactions on the Celo blockchain — including deposits, feeds, and withdrawals — are permanently and publicly visible on-chain. Anyone can query your wallet address and see your full interaction history with the Zorrito contracts. This is an inherent property of public blockchains and is outside our control.

We do not link your wallet address to any personal identity, and we do not attempt to de-anonymize users.

4. Analytics — PostHog

We use PostHog for product analytics. PostHog collects anonymous behavioral data: which pages you visit, which buttons you click, and which wallet type you use. No personal information is transmitted. Data is processed on PostHog's EU servers. You can learn more at posthog.com/privacy.

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any advertising trackers.

5. Third-Party Services

Zorrito integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Aave V3 — holds deposited USDT and generates yield on Celo
  • The Graph — indexes on-chain events for the public dashboard
  • WalletConnect / Reown — enables wallet connections via QR code or deep link
  • MiniPay / Lemon — wallet providers with their own data practices
  • Vercel — hosts the site; standard server logs may be retained briefly

We are not responsible for the data practices of any third-party service.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use localStorage (not cookies) to remember your language preference. No tracking cookies are set by Zorrito. PostHog may set a session cookie to associate analytics events within a session; this contains no personally identifiable information.

7. Data Retention

On-chain data is permanent by nature. Anonymous analytics events are retained by PostHog according to their retention policy. Language preferences stored in localStorage persist until you clear your browser data. We do not maintain any server-side database of user information.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personally identifiable information, most data subject rights (access, correction, deletion) do not apply in the traditional sense. If you have a question about data associated with your wallet address or analytics events, contact us via the official Zorrito channels and we will do our best to assist.

To opt out of PostHog analytics, you can use a browser ad-blocker or disable JavaScript for this site.

9. Children

Zorrito is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from minors. If you believe a minor has interacted with the Protocol, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
